# 图注与方法补充

## 中文方法段

对31省各180天的预测序列分别进行频谱分析。首先减去各序列均值，再通过最小二乘线性拟合去除趋势，随后施加周期型Hann窗以减轻有限序列截断引起的频谱泄漏。以每天一个样本计算单边FFT功率谱，并进行窗能量补偿。排除零频率分量后，将各频率格功率除以全部正频率功率之和，得到归一化功率谱。频率单位为cycles/day，频率分辨率为1/180 cycles/day，Nyquist频率为0.5 cycles/day。主导周期定义为非零频率最大功率峰对应频率的倒数。另按周期≥30天、7至小于30天、2至小于7天划分长、中、短周期频段，分别报告其占非零频率总功率的百分比。

## 中文图注

图7｜31省预测日碳排放序列及归一化功率谱。各省上图为提供的180天预测值，下图为去均值、去除线性趋势并施加Hann窗后的单边归一化功率谱。横轴频率单位为cycles/day，下图纵轴为每个频率格占全部非零频率总功率的百分比，采用统一对数刻度。橙色点表示最大功率峰，标题给出对应主导周期及长（L）、中（M）、短（H）周期频段的功率占比。L、M、H分别定义为周期≥30天、7至小于30天及2至小于7天；三个频段占比合计100%。这些指标刻画预测序列的频率结构，不是对真实排放周期的独立验证。

## English methods

Frequency analysis was performed separately for the 180-day predicted series of each of the 31 provinces. Each series was demeaned and detrended by removing its least-squares linear trend, then multiplied by a periodic Hann window to reduce spectral leakage caused by finite-record truncation. A one-sided FFT power spectrum was computed at one observation per day, with compensation for window energy. After excluding the zero-frequency component, each positive-frequency bin was normalized by the total positive-frequency power. Frequencies are reported in cycles/day, with a resolution of 1/180 cycles/day and a Nyquist frequency of 0.5 cycles/day. The dominant period was defined as the reciprocal of the nonzero frequency with the greatest power. Power fractions were also calculated for long (period ≥30 days), medium (7≤period<30 days), and short (2≤period<7 days) period bands.

## English caption

Figure 7 | Predicted daily carbon emissions and normalized power spectra for 31 provinces. For each province, the upper panel shows the supplied 180-day forecast and the lower panel shows its one-sided power spectrum after demeaning, linear detrending, and Hann windowing. Frequency is expressed in cycles/day. Spectral power is expressed as the percentage of total nonzero-frequency power per frequency bin and displayed on a common logarithmic scale. Orange markers indicate the maximum-power frequency; spectral panel titles report its dominant period and the long-, medium-, and short-period power fractions (L/M/H). The bands correspond to periods ≥30 days, 7 to <30 days, and 2 to <7 days, respectively, and their fractions sum to 100%. These descriptors characterize the model-generated forecasts and do not independently establish periodicity in observed emissions.
